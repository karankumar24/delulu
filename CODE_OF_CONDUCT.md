# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to do
something reasonable, and ask before you conclude otherwise.

## What that means here

Good: asking what someone meant, disagreeing with the idea, saying you do not
have time to review something, saying you were wrong.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, insults about who someone
is rather than what they wrote, sexual attention or advances, publishing
someone's private information, and following someone from thread to thread to
keep at them.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and anywhere else the
project happens.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to GitHub directly, through the Report abuse link on the comment or
account in question. GitHub can act on accounts, which is the part that actually
stops a problem, and it means you do not have to write to a stranger about
something unpleasant.

You can also open an issue if the matter is one you are comfortable discussing
in public. Do not open a public issue about harassment you are on the receiving
end of. Use the link above.

## What I will do

Remove comments that break this, and block accounts that keep doing it. I am one
person and I will not always see something quickly, so reporting it to GitHub is
usually faster than waiting for me.

I am held to this too. If I am the problem, GitHub's report link works on me as
well as on anyone else.
